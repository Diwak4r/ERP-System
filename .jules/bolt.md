## 2025-05-15 - [Django FormSet N+1 Optimization]
**Learning:** Django FormSets for data entry (like `ProductionEntry`) often suffer from N+1 query patterns because each form instance independently fetches `choices` for ForeignKey fields and performs individual lookups for business logic (like `TargetRule`). Pre-fetching these in the `BaseFormSet.__init__` and passing them via `get_form_kwargs` to the forms' `__init__` allows for O(1) lookups.
**Action:** When implementing or optimizing FormSets with ForeignKey fields, always use a `BaseFormSet` to bulk-fetch related data and choices, and pass them to individual forms to avoid redundant database round-trips. Always ensure the form's `queryset` is still correctly filtered for validation consistency even when `choices` are provided for rendering.
